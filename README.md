<p>These are jokes generally for kids, but may require a bit more imagination or thought to come up with the right answer on the first try. None of them would show up on a real test, so relax and enjoy with friends, or alone with some time to spare.</p>
<p>Here's a small sample of what's included...</p>
<div align="center">

Question | Answer
:-- | :--
You draw a line. Without touching it, how do you make the line longer? | You draw a shorter line next to it, and it becomes the longer line.
You see a boat full of people, yet there isn't a single person on board. How is that possible? | All the people on the boat are married.
If an electric train is going east at 60 miles an hour and there is a strong westerly wind, which way does the smoke from the train drift? | There is no smoke coming from electric trains.
